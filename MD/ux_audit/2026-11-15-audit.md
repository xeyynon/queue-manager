# UI/UX audit: all screens, 2026-11-15

**Audited for:** the queue manager at a counter (desktop or tablet, sometimes a phone) and the evaluator (desktop, demo login).
**Evidence:** the 42 screenshots in [2026-11-14-production/](2026-11-14-production/) (390/820/1280/1920 × light/dark), read alongside the code. I measured contrast with the WCAG formula against the tokens in `src/app/globals.css`.
**Not checked:** screen reader, a real phone, 200% zoom, slow network. No user test has been run.

Severity: **S1** loses or misstates data · **S2** blocks a task or breaks a playbook/project rule · **S3** friction · **S4** polish.

### Open questions (product decisions: yours to make, not mine)
- **Q-1 "Long wait" is fixed at 15 min** (`format.ts:37`). In the demo every waiting row is amber, which weakens the signal. Should each queue set its own threshold (for example OPD 30 min, Pharmacy 10 min)?
- **Q-2 The `A` shortcut adds an unnamed token at once.** A stray keypress creates a real token, and it counts in the dashboard's arrivals. Should `A` move focus to the name field instead, so Enter adds?
- **Q-3 On phones, "Now serving" is below the waiting list.** The docked bar shows who is *next* but not who is being served *now*. Should the docked bar show "Now #2824" too?

## Passed checks
- Status is never shown by colour alone: long wait = clock icon + "long wait" + amber (R-6).
- One primary (indigo) action per screen; Serve next is 64 px and docked on phones and tablets.
- Missing figures show "—" with a reason ("needs 5+ served (3 so far)"; "estimates appear after 3 serves in 2 h").
- Empty states explain what will appear, with one action.
- Dashboard filters live in the URL; charts have a "Show as table" alternative and `role="img"` labels.
- Delete queue: a two-step confirm with its consequence, and "Keep queue" focused. Cancel token: undo, no confirm.
- Offline state on the console is shown in words ("Offline · showing data from 12s ago").
- No AI-look patterns: no stripes, gradients, glass or tinted pills (`check-ai-look.sh`: no real hits).
- Page titles follow "View | Queue Manager".
